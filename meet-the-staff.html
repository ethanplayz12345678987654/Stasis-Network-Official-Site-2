Roles

Managers

Responsible for most community moderation, rule enforcement, and support. They can supervise staff, interview people, and act as admins.

  • Tobi — In-game and server moderation
  • Moderator2 — Community and staff manager

Administrators

Higher ranked Moderator but with extra permissions.

  • Admin 1 — Server admin
  • Admin 2 — Server admin

Moderators

Responsible for community moderation, rule enforcement, and support.

  • ArtOfLight — In-game and server moderation
  • Moderator2 — In-game and server moderation

Developers

Plugin & server development, feature work, and tooling.

  • Dev1 — Backend and scripts
  • Dev2 — Website and integrations

Helpers

Responsible for light community moderation, rule enforcement, and support. This is the entry-level moderation rank where people are promoted from.

  • Helper1 — In-game moderation
  • Helper2 — Community moderation

Advisors

Help the Owners with ideas and feedback from the community. Advisors are not always part of staff but may be consulted on decisions.

  • Advisor1 — Community feedback
  • Advisor2 — Community feedback

Executives

The top ranks of the network: Founder, Co-Founder, and Co-Owners who make high-level decisions.

Management Team

Top-level leadership and decision-makers for the network: Directors and Managers who coordinate teams and events.

Administrative Team

Admins and senior moderators who oversee moderation policies and server operations.

Moderation Team

Moderators who keep chats safe, enforce rules, and maintain community standards.

Development Team

Developers who build server features, plugins, and website integrations.
